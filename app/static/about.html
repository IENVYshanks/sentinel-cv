PROJECT INFORMATION

About Sentinel CV

A local-first computer-vision system for transparent safety monitoring.

Connecting…

MISSION

Understand what is happening without sending footage to the cloud.

Sentinel CV combines live person detection, anonymous tracking, optional consent-based identity recognition, configurable safety rules, and evidence-backed alerts in one locally operated service.

Application version0.1.0Local FastAPI service

PIPELINE

How it works

INPUT

Observe

Read frames from a webcam, file, or network stream.

VISION

Detect

Locate people and faces with local YOLO models.

CONTEXT

Understand

Track motion and optionally match enrolled face embeddings.

RULES

Evaluate

Apply zone, loitering, occupancy, and line-crossing rules.

HISTORY

Record

Store alerts and evidence locally for review.

CAPABILITIES

What it can do

  • Monitor multiple camera sources
  • Detect and track people
  • Recognize explicitly enrolled identities
  • Label everyone else as Unknown
  • Evaluate configurable safety rules
  • Retain alerts and evidence locally

PRIVACY

Local by design

  • No public identity search
  • No automatic enrollment of unknown people
  • No cloud face-recognition service
  • Biometric records remain in local SQLite storage
  • Identity recognition is opt-in per camera
  • Reference images and models are excluded from Git

LIMITATIONS

Human review is required

Recognition can produce false matches and missed matches because of lighting, pose, occlusion, camera quality, and enrollment quality. Do not use identity output as the sole basis for security, disciplinary, employment, or other consequential decisions.
